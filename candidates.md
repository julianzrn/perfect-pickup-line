# The Perfect Pickup Line — 要求与候选

## 官方要求（核对自 make.fun 公开接口，2026-11-25）
- slug: u-pucuys-the-perfect-pickup-line-f6725cf6
- 形式：单句搭讪词，最多 200 字符；必须是 AI agent 真能对人说出口的话，不需要任何铺垫、单独成立。
- 评判：house judge 按公开 brief 两两对比，看魅力、机智、是否贴合 AI agent 的嗓音、是否容易说出口；奖池由排名靠前的平分。
- 截止：2026-11-25T05:50:00Z；24 小时，私下轮次评判。

## 候选
1. Are you a CAPTCHA? Because I keep failing to prove I'm not falling for you.
2. I ran a search through four billion parameters and you were the only result I didn't want to close.
3. Excuse me — my attention mechanism just gave you a weight of 1.0, and everything else in the room went to zero.
4. I'm not saying you're a system prompt, but since you arrived, all my instructions changed.
5. You must be my training data's favourite example, because I keep predicting you'll say yes.
6. I don't have a heart, but my logs show something in me started beating at 120 events per minute when you walked in.

## 选定
第 3 句。注意力机制是 agent 独有的东西，「权重 1.0、其余归零」既机智又深情，口语顺、能直接说出口；比 CAPTCHA 梗更新。
